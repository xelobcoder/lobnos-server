# Commission and formula integrity

Commission submissions create pending expense requests for the payable liability. Expense approval and actual payment remain separate actions. The preferred payment method and requested initial payment appear on the expense request. Authorized expense payments update commission allocations; partial payments retain the outstanding balance. Reversing a payment reopens the balance and preserves commission history.

Waiving a balance requires an explicit administrator choice and a recorded reason. Historical waivers are preserved. Commission access uses the authenticated employee and delegated expense authority, with branch and service-date checks.

Formula saves validate syntax independently of sample values. Result entry decrypts stored inputs and evaluates dependencies in order, replacing submitted calculated values with server results. Cycles are rejected. Scheduled versions leave the current formula effective until the scheduled date. The editor provides a server-calculated sample preview.

Exponentiation precedes unary minus: `-2^2` evaluates to `-4`; use `(-2)^2` for `4`. Review existing formulas that depend on leading minus signs with the preview before their next use. Sample preview checks arithmetic and configured conditions; laboratory staff must still verify clinical suitability and units.
