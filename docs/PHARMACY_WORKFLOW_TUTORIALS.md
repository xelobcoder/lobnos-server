# PLS Pharmacy end-to-end tutorials

These are the eight required task paths. The online manual must link to the precise page procedures at each stage, retain role restrictions and never perform a transaction automatically.

| Tutorial | Actual sequence | Permissions and success evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Sell a medicine | Cashier & Shift > Point of Sale > direct payment/receipt OR counter invoice > Counter invoices payment > medication collection | Selling role; counter payment requires manager/cashier/accountant and handover requires authorized dispensing; successful receipt and collection state |
| Request new stock | Stock Levels > Inventory & purchasing > Submit purchase request > Purchase requests | Stock role; request appears awaiting independent review; stock does not change |
| Purchase medicines | Purchase orders > New purchase order > supplier, medicines and quantities only > Save draft or Submit for approval > approve > choose PDF/email/SMS > Receive medicines > enter actual buying costs, counts and expiry > optionally Change selling price beside each medicine > Save stock (or Save stock & supplier bill) > Continue to payment or Pay later | Blank batch numbers and delivery references are generated automatically. Routine receiving needs no reason. Prices stay unchanged unless explicitly adjusted by an authorized manager using exact, amount or percentage methods on the same screen. Missing invoices can be deferred explicitly. Unpaid posted invoices remain Accounts Payable; accepted but unbilled stock is not a fabricated supplier bill. Payment still requires independent approval. |
| Pay a supplier | Original purchase order workspace > Supplier payments > original invoice balance > Pay supplier > explicit full/partial allocation > submit > independent approve > pay > review remaining balance/history | Money role; existing controlled settlement workflow remains in the workspace. Invoice-level allocations prevent paying the wrong bill; combined invoice balances are labelled as full invoice balances. Not an automated bank transfer. Older receipt-backed bills still use Payables. |
| Record a pharmacy expense | Expenses > New expense draft > detail evidence > submit > independent approve > pay > payments/evidence | Money role; immutable cost, approved status, actual payment and remaining balance; do not enter stock purchases here |
| Collect money owed by a customer | Customer accounts & invoices > patient > Invoices / collect > invoice > Record collection > receipt | Existing authorized invoice collection; received amount/method/reference, original outstanding balance reduced; no second sale |
| Review pharmacy profit | Financial statements > Profit & loss > dates > Apply filters > revenue/cost/expense rows > source ledger | Money role; applied period agrees with source records; report reading/export does not post |
| Correct a transaction | Identify original record > sales reversal OR unpaid counter cancellation OR controlled financial reversal/refund > independent review when required > verify original and correction evidence | Management for sale reversal; Money for controlled documents; never delete history or duplicate a sale/payment to fix it |

Detailed field instructions and verification must be supplied by the linked online page guides. An end-to-end tutorial is not permission to bypass approval, period locks, stock safety, clinical review or ambiguous-payment retry controls.

Routine receiving uses one atomic `POST /api/v1/pharmacy/receiving/receive` operation for delivery evidence, eligible stock, optional price changes and an optional supplier invoice. Price previews use the same server-side quantity conversion, landed-cost allocation, order limits and rounding policy as saving. A rejected stock, price or invoice check rolls back the entire operation; uncertain outcomes must retry the original request key.

All receiving actions open `/pharmacy/inventory/receive`, a dedicated full-width page with desktop and mobile sidebars, sidebar toggles and ribbon navigation hidden. Order and medicine links preselect the relevant context; existing receipts load through branch-scoped `GET /api/v1/pharmacy/receiving/deliveries/:id`, including older receipts outside the latest-200 list. Back navigation restores the normal workspace; Continue to payment opens the order's payment section or supplier payables.

Use **Save physical delivery only** when cost is unknown or goods must remain held; it adds no usable stock and applies no prices or invoice. Actual purchase cost is required for valued stock, and tracked medicines still require a real unexpired expiry date. Use the manufacturer's batch number when available; generated internal numbers do not replace manufacturer recall evidence. Excess approval, returns and policy changes still require the appropriate manager authority and reason. Routine ordered-versus-actual cost differences are recorded automatically in the acceptance audit event.
