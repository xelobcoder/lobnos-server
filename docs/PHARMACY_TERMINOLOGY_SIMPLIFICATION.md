# Pharmacy terminology

Use ordinary language first and retain formal terminology as a subtitle or explanation. Do not rename database fields, accounting APIs or stored account identities.

| Reader-facing phrase | Formal term | Practical meaning and action |
|---|---|---|
| Money customers owe us | Accounts receivable | An unpaid sale is a customer balance. Collect against the original invoice; do not sell it twice. |
| Money we owe suppliers | Accounts payable / supplier liability | Received stock can create an amount owed. Match the bill before approving a settlement. |
| All pharmacy accounting transactions | General ledger | Trace a balance to its source; do not edit posted history. |
| Money categories | Chart of accounts | Accountant-managed buckets for sales, stock, costs and balances. |
| Shared accounting records | Shared ledger | Optional linked facility posting; pharmacy records stay identifiable. Check configuration and reconciliation before claiming consolidation. |
| Cost of medicines sold | Cost of goods sold | Selling GH20 of medicine that cost GH12 produces GH12 stock cost, not GH20. Check the original receipt if cost is wrong. |
| Compare statement and records | Reconciliation | Match bank lines to recorded money movements; do not collect again. |
| Accountant adjustment | Journal entry | Balanced debit/credit lines for reviewed corrections, not routine sales. |
| Account balance check | Trial balance | Debits/credits may balance even when the wrong account was used. Investigate sources. |
| Accounting date window | Financial period | Organization-controlled posting dates; not the cashier shift or trading day. |
| What stock is worth | Inventory valuation | Quantity and stock cost, not retail selling value. Compare with the accounting control. |
| Record now, pay later | Accrual | Recognize the cost and amount owed before payment, as the approved workflow requires. |
| Two sides of an entry | Debit and credit | Not universal words for money in/out; the account type determines their effect. |
| Reviewed reduction of a balance | Credit note | Evidence-backed correction of the original invoice, not a fresh receipt. |
| Delivery evidence | Goods received note / stock receipt | Records what arrived. Approval alone never adds stock. |

Each relevant article supplies a scenario, when the term appears, why it exists and the user's next action. Advanced shared-posting language belongs in authorized setup, not the default daily cash workflow.
